# Review Round 12 — Response to the faculty review (pre-submission)

Reviewer's overall recommendation: make the real-world alloy-discovery problem the central contribution and keep FCP as the methodological innovation.

| # | Comment (severity) | Response | Where |
|---|---|---|---|
